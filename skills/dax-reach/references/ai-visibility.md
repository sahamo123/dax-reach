# AI Visibility

Combines Corey's brand/answer analysis with AgriciDaniel's platform-access review. See Google G01, G11, G12 and the current source registry.

Separate four outcomes: brand mentioned, source cited, brand recommended, and qualified visit/lead. Record exact prompt, platform/product, available model identifier, date, locale, search/browsing mode, answer, citations, and trial count. A single response is an observation, not market share or a rank. If live querying is unavailable, deliver a readiness review and an explicit measurement plan.

For Google, inspect current indexing/snippet eligibility and the Search Console Search generative AI inclusion setting when authorized access exists. Do not infer that setting from HTML. The dedicated Generative AI performance report measures impressions; distinguish it from general search clicks and conversion data. The reference registry explains a conflict between an older overview and newer dedicated documentation.

For other engines, verify their own official search access and crawler guidance. Never require training access as a presumed condition for search discovery. Do not copy Google's rules into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini app, or Perplexity claims without evidence. This package does not require a specific bot allowlist.

Check that the site's real audience, service, process, limitations, author/business identity, and relevant proof are understandable. Keep answers and contextual links in accessible page content. Improve genuine case evidence, comparisons, useful images/video, and consistent verified profiles where they serve the audience. Do not invent reviews or publish artificial forum mentions. Social links are destinations for real supporting information, not an automatic visibility boost.

Use clear answers and sensible headings for readers; there is no required answer-block word count. Google does not use llms.txt for visibility. Consider optional machine-readable exports only for a named consumer or measured experiment, with no Google ranking claim.

Output an evidence table, any inaccurate AI descriptions with their observations, a prioritized content/access plan, and a repeatable prompt sample. Keep experiments and unverified platform theories labeled. Preserve useful website design; avoid turning a homepage into a large keyword or FAQ dump.
